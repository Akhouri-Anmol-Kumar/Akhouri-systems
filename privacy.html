PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

Akhouri Systems ("we", "us", "our") builds free Windows desktop software — ATLOCK, ANOTE, ACALCU and APIC. This page explains what happens with your data when you visit this website or use our applications.

1. Our desktop software

Our applications are designed to run locally on your machine. They do not collect, transmit, or upload your files, passwords, photos, or usage data to any server we control. Any data our apps create (such as ATLOCK's encrypted password vault or intruder logs) stays on your device.

2. This website

This website (akhouri-anmol-kumar.github.io) is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as our hosting provider, may automatically log standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes. We do not have access to this raw server data beyond aggregate GitHub Pages traffic statistics.

3. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on this site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on someone's prior visits to our site or other sites on the internet.
  • If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent banner allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies before ads are personalized.
  • You can learn more about how Google uses data at Google's Partner Sites policy.

4. Downloads

Our software is distributed via GitHub Releases. When you click a download link, your browser connects directly to GitHub's servers (github.com), which is subject to GitHub's own Privacy Statement.

5. Contact form

If you use the contact section on our homepage, any information you choose to submit is sent directly and is only used to respond to your message. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties.

6. Children's privacy

Our website and software are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, reach out via the contact section on our homepage.
